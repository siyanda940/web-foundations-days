About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple note-taking tool that helps users organise their thoughts, tasks and important information. It makes creating and managing notes quick and easy.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open QuickNotes and create a new note.
  2. Enter your important information or ideas.
  3. Save your note so you can find it later.

Features

  • Easy note-taking
  • Simple and user-friendly design
  • Organised information
  • Quick access to notes

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Ctrl + N Create a new note
Ctrl + S Save your note
